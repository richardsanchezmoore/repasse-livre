Long before dating apps…

Scripture was already teaching women to watch a man's character. "Charm is deceptive, and beauty is fleeting" (Prov. 31:30). The types haven't changed:

  • The smooth talker — full of words, empty of deeds.
  • The sluggard — grand dreams, little diligence.
And chances are, you've already met one.
Why they're so hard to spot

They rarely introduce themselves by name.

  • "I only worry because I care about you."starts as care, turns into control.
  • "You're not like other girls."all charm, no follow-through.
  • "One day I'll turn my life around."big dreams, little diligence.

The heart is charmed by words.
Wisdom watches the pattern.
